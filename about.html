About Hope of Living

Hope of Living is a community-focused organisation based in Lower Philipp, Cape Town. The organisation aims to support people in the community and create positive opportunities for individuals and families.

We believe that every person deserves hope, dignity and access to support. Through our activities and services, we work towards making a positive difference in the lives of people in our community.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide support, encouragement and opportunities that help individuals and families build better lives.

Our Vision

Our vision is to see a stronger and more supportive community where people have hope and opportunities for a better future.
